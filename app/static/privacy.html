Privacy

Your data, kept simple

Last updated: 2026

The short version, up front — then the details.

Short version. OmniDL is a hosted service. To download something, the link or search you submit is processed on our servers. We keep standard server logs and basic, aggregate analytics to run and protect the site. There are no accounts, and we don't sell your data.

What we process

When you start a download, your request goes to our server, which fetches the requested audio or video and returns the file to your browser. To do that — and to keep the service running and abuse-free — we handle:

  • Request data — the link or search you submit, the format/quality you choose, and timestamps, while your job is processed.
  • Server logs — standard technical logs such as your IP address, browser user-agent, and the requests made, used for operation, security and troubleshooting.
  • Analytics — aggregate usage statistics (e.g. page views and feature use) so we can understand traffic and improve OmniDL.

What we don't do

No accounts and no passwords. We don't sell or rent your personal data, and we don't use it to build advertising profiles.

On your device

OmniDL stores your theme choice and a couple of UI preferences in your browser's local storage. Files you download are saved by your browser to wherever you choose.

Third-party services

To fulfil a download, our server connects to the relevant services (e.g. YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify's public pages) to fetch content. Those services have their own privacy policies and may log the requests. OmniDL is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them.

Cookies

OmniDL needs no login cookies to work. Our analytics may set a cookie or similar identifier to measure traffic; you can block these in your browser without affecting downloads.

Data retention

Processed media, server logs and analytics are kept only as long as needed to provide and secure the service, then routinely removed.

Changes

We may update this policy as OmniDL evolves. Material changes will be reflected here, with a new date above.
