Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Short version: OmniDL runs entirely on your own computer. There is no OmniDL server, no account, and no analytics. We never see your activity or your files.

What we collect

Nothing. OmniDL has no backend service operated by us and sends no telemetry. It does not transmit your searches, links, downloads, or settings to any OmniDL-run server — because there isn't one.

Data stored on your device

To work, OmniDL keeps a few files locally on your machine:

  • Settings (config.json) — your output folder, format and preferences, including any optional Spotify credentials you enter.
  • History (history.json) — your recent download jobs and logs, so they survive a restart.
  • Theme choice — stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Your downloaded audio — saved to the folder you choose.

These never leave your computer. Delete them at any time to clear that data.

Third-party services

When you download, OmniDL connects directly from your device to the relevant services (e.g. YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify's public pages) to fetch content. Those services have their own privacy policies and may log requests coming from your IP address, exactly as a browser would. OmniDL is not affiliated with any of them.

Cookies

OmniDL itself sets no tracking cookies. If you optionally provide a browser cookie file to improve download reliability, it is used only locally to authenticate your own requests.

Changes

Because OmniDL is local software, "privacy" is mostly a matter of what the app does on your machine. Any change to that behaviour will be reflected on this page.
