About OmniDL

A unified music & video downloader.

OmniDL grew out of a simple frustration: the free tools that let you keep a personal copy of your music kept breaking. Spotify locked its Web API behind Premium, downloaders scattered across half a dozen command-line scripts, and none of them were pleasant to use. OmniDL pulls it all together into one clean dashboard.

What it does

Paste a Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud link (or just type a search) and OmniDL figures out the best official-audio source, downloads it with yt-dlp, and writes proper metadata and cover art onto the file. For Spotify it reads the public playlist page and matches each track to its studio master — no Premium and no developer credentials required. Prefer the full video? Flip the Audio / Video toggle and OmniDL grabs it as an MP4 (up to 4K) or MKV.

How it's built

OmniDL is a FastAPI app with a live WebSocket terminal, orchestrating yt-dlp, scdl and the Spotify resolver. The dashboard does the matching, downloading and tagging for you and streams the progress back live.

No friction. No Premium, no developer keys, and no sign-up — paste a link and go. OmniDL is built for personal use; please support the artists you love.

Fair use

OmniDL is intended for personal use — backing up music you have the right to, and supporting the artists you love. Please respect the terms of the services you use and the rights of content creators. See our Terms and Privacy pages.
