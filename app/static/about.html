About OmniDL

One tool for your whole library

OmniDL grew out of a simple frustration: the free tools for keeping a personal copy of your music kept breaking. Spotify locked its Web API behind Premium, and the good downloaders were scattered across a dozen command-line scripts. OmniDL pulls it all into one clean dashboard.

What it does

Paste a Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud link (or just type a search) and OmniDL finds the best official source, downloads it, and writes proper metadata and cover art onto the file — no Premium and no developer keys required.

🎧

Spotify without Premium

Reads the public playlist page and fetches each track's official audio — no login, no API, no 403s.

✨

Official-audio matching

Matches every track to its studio master by artist, title and length — the clean version, not a random video.

🎬

Music or full video

One toggle switches between clean audio and the full video — MP4 up to 4K, or MKV.

How it's built

OmniDL is a FastAPI app with a live WebSocket terminal that orchestrates the tools the community trusts. The dashboard handles matching, downloading and tagging for you and streams the progress back in real time.
