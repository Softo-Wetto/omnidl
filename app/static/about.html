About OmniDL

A unified, local-first music downloader.

OmniDL grew out of a simple frustration: the free tools that let you keep a personal copy of your music kept breaking. Spotify locked its Web API behind Premium, downloaders scattered across half a dozen command-line scripts, and none of them were pleasant to use. OmniDL pulls it all together into one clean, self-hosted dashboard.

What it does

Paste a Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud link (or just type a search) and OmniDL figures out the best official-audio source, downloads it with yt-dlp, and writes proper metadata and cover art onto the file. For Spotify it reads the public playlist page and matches each track to its studio master — no Premium and no developer credentials required.

How it's built

OmniDL is a small FastAPI app with a live WebSocket terminal, orchestrating yt-dlp, scdl and the Spotify resolver. It runs entirely on your machine — bound to 127.0.0.1 — and can be packaged into a single standalone application that needs no Python install.

Local by design. OmniDL has no servers, no accounts, and no telemetry. Everything happens on your computer, and your downloads never leave it.

Fair use

OmniDL is intended for personal use — backing up music you have the right to, and supporting the artists you love. Please respect the terms of the services you use and the rights of content creators. See our Terms and Privacy pages.
